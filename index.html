Programs and admissions

What we offer and how to start.

Senior High: Academic track

  • ABM: Accountancy, Business and Management
  • GAS: General Academic Strand
  • HUMSS: Humanities and Social Sciences
  • DepEd SHS Permits No. 789 s. 2016 and No. 113 s. 2017

Senior High: Tech-Voc track

Tech-Voc specializations appear here once the school confirms them. Like the Academic track: no registration, tuition or miscellaneous fee, as advertised.

TESDA courses

  • Computer Systems Servicing NC II, 280 hours
  • Electronic Products Assembly and Servicing NC II, 260 hours
  • Dressmaking NC II, 275 hours
  • Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) NC I and NC II, 268 hours each
  • Each course also runs as a Mobile Training Program

What to prepare

How to apply

  1. Apply onlineChoose a Senior High strand or a TESDA course and get a reference number.
  2. Prepare the requirementsThe registrar sends the list by Messenger or text.
  3. Visit the officeLiatsan Family Bldg., MacArthur Highway, San Sebastian, Tarlac City.
  4. Start your classesBegin Senior High or your TESDA training.
